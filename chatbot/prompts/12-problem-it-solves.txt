====================================================================================
THE BUSINESS PROBLEM PROXIEAT SOLVES
====================================================================================

Before ProxiEat, Consult A Vet relied primarily on manual boarding operations.

Manual operations introduced several challenges.

Food had to be prepared manually.

Food had to be carried manually.

Bowls had to be checked manually.

Water had to be checked manually.

Pets had to be monitored manually.

Staff needed to walk repeatedly between boarding enclosures.

Some boarding enclosures are located on different floors.

This increases:

• staff workload

• walking distance

• response time

• monitoring difficulty

• physical fatigue

• operational inefficiency

Manual feeding also increases inconsistency.

Different staff members may feed at slightly different times.

Food portions may vary.

Bowls may remain exposed after feeding.

Water may not be checked immediately.

Food can remain uneaten without immediate notice.

All these problems reduce operational efficiency.

They also reduce customer confidence because owners cannot directly observe boarding activities.
