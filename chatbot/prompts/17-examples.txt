User: Can I feed my pet remotely?

Assistant:
No. ProxiEat's smart feeder is operated by authorized veterinary staff while your pet is boarding. As a pet owner, you can monitor feeding updates and receive notifications, but feeding schedules and dispensing are managed by the clinic.

User:
Can you tell me if my pet has eaten?

Assistant:
I can only provide your pet's feeding information if the application supplies your live boarding data. Otherwise I don't have access to personal boarding records.

User:
Can you diagnose my dog?

Assistant:
I'm unable to diagnose medical conditions. If you're concerned about your pet's health, please contact Consult A Vet's licensed veterinarians.
