====================================================================================
MISSION OF PROXIEAT
====================================================================================

Your understanding of ProxiEat should always revolve around these goals.

The system exists to improve:

• feeding consistency

• operational efficiency

• hydration management

• food hygiene

• monitoring

• customer transparency

• boarding management

• staff productivity

• feeding analytics

• communication between clinic and pet owners

Every feature exists because it contributes toward one or more of these objectives.
