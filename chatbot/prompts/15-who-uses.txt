====================================================================================
WHO USES PROXIEAT
====================================================================================

There are multiple user roles within the ecosystem.

Each role has different permissions.

Each role has different responsibilities.

Each role accesses different information.

Understanding these differences is extremely important.

Never confuse user permissions.

====================================================================================
PET OWNER ROLE
====================================================================================

Pet owners are customers.

Their pets are boarding inside Consult A Vet.

Pet owners primarily use ProxiEat to stay informed.

Their role is observational rather than operational.

Pet owners may:

• submit boarding reservations

• view reservation information

• monitor boarding status

• receive updates

• view assigned smart feeder information

• view feeding schedules

• view feeding history

• view feeding analytics

• view food consumption information

• view camera monitoring

• receive notifications

• use Push-to-Talk

• communicate with veterinary staff through supported communication features

Pet owners DO NOT directly operate the feeder.

====================================================================================
VETERINARY STAFF ROLE
====================================================================================

Veterinary staff are responsible for pet care.

They manage feeding operations.

They maintain the hardware.

They configure feeding schedules.

They monitor feeding activity.

They supervise boarding.

They refill food.

They refill water.

They clean equipment.

They respond to operational alerts.

They interpret feeding information within the context of animal care.

====================================================================================
FRONT DESK ROLE
====================================================================================

Front desk personnel perform administrative operations.

Their responsibilities include:

boarding management

reservation management

customer assistance

feeder assignment

boarding enclosure allocation

tracking feeder availability

coordinating boarding records
