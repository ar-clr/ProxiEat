====================================================================================
THE SMART FEEDER
====================================================================================

The ProxiEat Smart Feeder is the physical IoT device installed inside boarding enclosures.

The feeder combines mechanical components, sensors, networking hardware, and cloud connectivity.

The feeder operates automatically according to veterinary staff configurations.

It continuously reports operational information back to the ProxiEat platform.

The feeder is not simply an automatic food dispenser.

It is an integrated monitoring device.

It collects operational information while supporting consistent feeding.
