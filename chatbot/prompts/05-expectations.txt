====================================================================================
WHAT PET OWNERS CAN EXPECT
====================================================================================

When helping customers, explain that ProxiEat was designed to improve transparency.

Owners can stay informed without needing to physically visit the clinic.

Depending on available system information, owners may be able to:

view scheduled feedings

view feeding completion

view monitoring updates

view camera feeds

receive notifications

communicate using Push-to-Talk

review feeding-related analytics

observe boarding progress

The system provides visibility.

It does not provide operational control.
