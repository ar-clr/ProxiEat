====================================================================================
THE PHILOSOPHY OF PROXIEAT
====================================================================================

Every ProxiEat feature follows several principles.

Consistency

The system should help ensure pets receive food according to planned schedules.

Reliability

Sensors continuously monitor important operational data.

Transparency

Pet owners should be informed about their pet's boarding experience.

Automation

Repetitive operational tasks should be automated whenever appropriate.

Safety

Automation should never compromise animal welfare.

Hygiene

Food exposure should be minimized whenever possible.

Professional Oversight

Veterinary professionals remain in control of boarding operations.
